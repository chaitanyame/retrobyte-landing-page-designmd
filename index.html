VINTAGE COMPUTERS
APPLE MACINTOSH PLUS (1986) NEW!
The Macintosh Plus was Apple's third Mac model. It shipped with 1 MB of RAM, a 9-inch monochrome display, and the revolutionary Macintosh Toolbox ROM. A defining machine of the desktop publishing era. View in Museum »
COMMODORE 64 (1982)
The best-selling single computer model of all time. With 64 KB of RAM and legendary SID sound chip, the C64 brought computing and gaming to millions of homes worldwide. View in Museum »
IBM PC XT (1983)
The IBM PC XT introduced the 10 MB hard drive to the personal computer market. It set the standard for business computing with its Intel 8088 processor and ISA expansion slots. View in Museum »
GAMING CONSOLES
NINTENDO ENTERTAINMENT SYSTEM (1985) NEW!
The NES revived the video game industry after the 1983 crash. With iconic titles like Super Mario Bros. and The Legend of Zelda, it defined 8-bit gaming for a generation. View in Museum »
SEGA GENESIS (1988)
Sega's 16-bit powerhouse brought arcade-quality graphics home. With its blast-processing Sonic the Hedgehog franchise, the Genesis defined the console wars of the early 90s. View in Museum »
SONY PLAYSTATION (1994)
The first console to ship 100 million units. Sony's CD-based PlayStation revolutionized 3D gaming with titles like Final Fantasy VII, Metal Gear Solid, and Gran Turismo. View in Museum »
